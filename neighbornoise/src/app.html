Neighbor Noise

Write it down. Every time.

A free log for noise from the neighbours. No app, no equipment in your living room, no appointment. Note what you heard and when — and get one tidy overview you can hand on.

No account, no email address. Your postcode and house number are the key — come back any time, on any device.

How it works

01

Open your log

Postcode and house number, nothing else. That address is your log.

02

Add a line each time

Date and time are already filled in. Write what you heard. Tick the box if you were not at home.

03

Hand the overview on

Download it as a spreadsheet or print it to PDF, and give it to your landlord, housing association or council.

Why this is different

Start in ten seconds

No quote, no sales call, no two-week waiting list. Type your address and you are logging.

Nothing in your home

No measuring case on a tripod, nobody coming round to place it or collect it.

You do not need permission

You do not have to go through your landlord or the council to begin. The record is yours.

Note when you were away

Noise while your home stood empty cannot be yours. It is the one line most logs forget.

What this is, and what it is not

This is a logbook, not a sound measurement. It records no decibels, and we claim nothing about what will hold up in court.

What it does give you is the part that is usually missing: dates and times, written down the same way every time, over weeks — instead of reconstructed from memory months later.

Whether that is enough depends on who reads it. Finding out costs you nothing.

About privacy

Your log sits under your postcode and house number, so anyone who types that address can read it. Write nothing you would mind the neighbours seeing — and ask us and we delete the lot.
